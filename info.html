Fluid Simulation

This simulation solves the Navier-Stokes equations for incompressible fluids. The fluid visualization includes thousands of Lagrangian particles that follow the velocity field and leave behind semi-transparent trails as they move. All computation happens in several GPU fragment shaders for real-time performance. Based on work by Amanda Ghassaei, built with her gpu-io library.

Instructions: touch or move your mouse to apply a force to the fluid. Press "p" to save a screenshot.
