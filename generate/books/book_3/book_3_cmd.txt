#windows
$batwin335
*HOW TO CHECK BATTERY LIFE OF LAPTOP IN WINDOWS
(open cmd in administration)
-powercfg /energy
(this cmd saves a file energy-report.html, open this file and find "Design Capacity" and "Last Full Charge")
FORMULA:
BATTERY HEALTH = (LAST FULL CHARGE / DESIGN CAPACITY) x 100
FOR EX:
DESIGN CAPACITY = 35300
LAST FULL CHARGE = 29060
BH = 29060 / 35300 x 100 = 82%
(if your battery health is below 80% then it's time to change your battery)
